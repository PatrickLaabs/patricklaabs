# About me

I'm Patrick, a DevOps engineer and Go developer who builds and runs cloud-native infrastructure. Most of my work happens around Kubernetes and Cluster API: provisioning clusters declaratively, writing controllers, and keeping platforms boring in the best possible way. Always learning, always caffeinated ☕.

Since 2022 I've been a DevOps engineer at EITCO in Bonn, where I design and build our in-house Kubernetes platform. I live near Bonn, Germany.
